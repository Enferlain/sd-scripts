## 4. Required older-cut recovery check — K/A1, retirement at A2

This resolves the question routed by Passes 5–6 at the level the existing requirements actually establish.

**Premises:** K is a coherent captured snapshot at accepted scope A1 with live participant P. Later accepted scope A2 retires P and may declare Q at the same authored address. Later effects/history have some known and some uncertain outcomes. Recovery requests the same logical run under a new execution session.

| Question | Established requirements and derived consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Was K originally valid? | Its original capture/coverage/publication claim is judged against its supported cut and evidence at A1. Later resource loss, retirement, or unavailable reconstruction does not rewrite that historical outcome. Capture and restore readiness are separate. [CC-cut] [CC-publication] [FM-artifacts] |
| May the new session revive P? | No. **Retired reference remains historical** says the reference is never revived or reassigned. The same-run session change preserves logical identity; it does not reset lifecycle history. Restoring K's live P as current after the established retirement would violate that rule. [RS-continuity] [RS-resume] [CC-restore] |
| May it replace P with Q because the address matches? | No. Authored address is not incarnation equality. Q is a different reference, and K's members/relationships/state mappings refer to P. Neither address reuse nor physical reconstruction establishes a mapping or permits an invented substitution. [RS-identity] [OU-members] [CC-restore] |
| Must K therefore be erased or marked never captured? | No. Keep its actual A1 guarantee and resources/history. The present same-run restoration request cannot publish K's live-P state under these premises; report the incompatible/unsatisfied continuation and keep dependent use unavailable. This is later ineligibility, not retrospective capture failure. [CC-publication] [CC-failed] |
| What happens to A2 and delayed facts? | Keep the accepted retirement, Q if declared, and known later effects under their original scope/session/outcome. | What about post-cut known/uncertain effects? | Preserve known first-unit/reached outcomes separately from uncertain later calls/external effects. New session identity does not resolve effect uncertainty. [CC-effects] [CC-restore] |

**Conclusion:** the requirements already admit a coherent supported response: **do not restore this ineligible K as current same-run state; preserve its historical capture and the run's later lifecycle/effect history, and leave affected continuation unavailable.** A later eligible coherent cut may be used if one actually exists and its required coverage/correspondence is established; no such cut is assumed here. No rollback, branching, identity substitution or automatic artifact fallback is invented.

This does **not** mean every older snapshot is unusable. Where an earlier cut conflicts with no permanent lifecycle constraint and accepted reconciliation can establish the requested continuation, the conditional restoration requirements leave that case available. Nor does the audit assert that every numerical effect after an earlier cut must be preserved as current weights: what must remain preserved is the truthful effect/history knowledge and the policy's required continuation correspondence. An accepted snapshot does not itself authorize ignoring later known or uncertain effects.

There is no source offer of universal restoration from every historical K that forces a contradiction here. If a future product explicitly wants to resume an A1 branch with live P despite terminal same-run retirement, that is an additional recovery/identity policy needing an explicit architectural decision; G5 storage selection cannot quietly grant it. It is not a prerequisite for the narrower conditional support already accepted. **No new P7 finding is assigned for this case.**
