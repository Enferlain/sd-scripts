## 3. Capture, dependencies and actual completion

### 3.1 Capture retains source meaning

The artifact exchange distinguishes declaration/request, semantic planning, capture, publication and actual results. This is a semantic distinction, not a mandatory five-function API or universal execution sequence. Coverage follows the declared use, not a universal state dictionary. Teacher/student/EMA selection, adapters and their hosts, auxiliary learned state, construction facts and transformations must preserve the meaning actually selected. A training dependency is not automatically a consumer dependency, and historical initialization provenance is not automatically the current consumer base. [Artifact coverage][artifact-coverage] [Product design][product-design]

Stable capture must protect the selected arrangement, topology, relationships, numerical values and required owner state through actual source reads and backend completion. Borrowed live tensors alone do not establish stability. A later update or topology transition may invalidate a live view while leaving an already independent stable capture valid. Serialization/upload may then finish after that transition, with the earlier source meaning. A stronger policy requiring latest state must be explicit. [Artifact capture][artifact-capture] [Preparation][preparation] [Protected use][protected-use]

Export conversion, quantization, merge/fold and other derived output must be declared and describe the resulting product. Export alone must not silently mutate the accepted running arrangement. If a selected export performs live effects, those effects and cleanup belong to the governed transition/recovery rules. Cleanup failure can leave execution unavailable while previously written output remains known. [Artifact transformation/effects][artifact-transform]

### 3.2 Completion depends on declared use and policy

A semantic member may span several shards, and one resource may hold several members. Shared resources and optional constituents prevent file count or a single path from defining product completeness. Missing required members, construction state, manifests, shards or destinations remain unsatisfied even when another resource is durable. Optional omissions must be reported rather than silently changing the declaration. [Member/resource coverage][artifact-members] [Actual results][artifact-results]

An adapter that needs the **updated** base is not made valid by naming only the original initialization base. Consumer dependency identity and compatibility must follow the selected product meaning. A reference-only completion policy can accept a stable compatible base reference without embedding or currently fetching it. A policy requiring an available/resolvable dependency cannot. Thus complete, usable now and self-contained are different claims. This is explicit, rather than an assumption supplied by this audit. [Updated base][artifact-base] [Reference policies][artifact-results]

### 3.3 Success, partial effects and reporting remain distinguishable

The result must report measured post-write resources, formats, sizes, checksums and their scope, references, omissions, failures and uncertainty. The plan cannot substitute for these facts. An exception or absent return does not prove that no files or remote effects exist. Local completion, required publication and observation have separate outcomes; no atomic multi-resource publication or deletion-on-failure guarantee is inferred. [Actual results][artifact-results]

“Reporting fails after publication” is consistent with required completion obligations. Successfully written/published payload remains a known authoritative outcome; reporting must not be described as successful. If a particular accepted policy makes a sidecar, provenance contribution or index part of completion, failure of that required constituent still leaves **that completion claim** unsatisfied. Preserving known output does not waive it. Metadata observation also cannot roll back an accepted live transition. [Publication/reporting][artifact-results] [Required composition filing][companion-family] [Observation ownership][metadata-design]

Retention follows actual resource membership and outstanding accepted use. An upload still reading a resource prevents deletion/overwrite until completion or accepted cancellation releases access; shared membership matters. Restoring or otherwise reading a resource requires the corresponding actual-use protection. This derives from the shared lifetime rules; it does not require a second, snapshot-specific ownership architecture. No rule promises permanent retention of every external reference. [Retention][artifact-results] [Cache resource lifetime][cache-lifetime] [Protected use][protected-use]

## 4. Runtime recovery and unfinished work

### 4.1 A coherent cut is relational

The continuation policy identifies required contributions and consistency relationships. Owners may have different coordinates: an alternating discriminator/generator boundary, a lagging producer policy or separate capability clock can be valid. What matters is that their saved positions agree under accepted handoff, consumption, advancement, revision and effect relationships. A saved global step is neither required nor sufficient. Required rank contributions must belong to that cut; durable but incompatible shards do not satisfy it. [Snapshot cut][snapshot-cut] [G4.4][snapshot-design]

The unfinished-work requirements cover produced, unpublished/ready, admitted, handed-off, consumed, contributed, advanced and durable distinctions where continuation depends on them. Required gradients, pending contribution windows, retained numerical versions, adaptive state/RNG, producer state, installed transformations and external interactions must be preserved or reconstructible under an accepted rule. If that cannot be established, capture waits for a supported boundary or reports coverage unavailable. [Unfinished recovery][unfinished] [Optimization continuation][optimization] [Execution effects][execution-effects]

This does not require persisting every queue, autograd graph or process object. Disposable unconsumed work can be regenerated only where captured identity, dependency provenance and consumption/advancement correspondence support that rule. Already consumed input cannot become unused again while restoring an optimizer position that includes it. Merely pausing a producer or saving its cursor/index is insufficient. [Coverage/reconstruction][snapshot-cut] [Cache continuation][cache-lifetime] [Input ownership][input-execution]

### 4.2 Unknown effects cannot become a retry authorization

For a first unit with a known advancement outcome and a second unit with an uncertain outcome, recovery preserves both facts. Neither a missing record nor a returned optimizer call proves that no numerical/backend effect occurred. It cannot authorize blind replay or manufacture coherence from the uncertain live state. The supported response is an evidenced valid position, a supported recovery rule, or an eligible earlier coherent snapshot. [Unfinished recovery][unfinished] [Optimization failure][optimization] [G4.4 failure][snapshot-design]

The bounded two-pass profile explicitly excludes mid-perturbation capture. A clean visible parameter tensor is not sufficient restoration evidence if gradients, scaler, master/sharded/offloaded state or other effects remain uncertain. Actual backend support determines readiness and safe handback. An unsupported profile can remain unavailable; researching it does not obligate G5 to support every backend combination. [Imperative authority][imperative] [Protected use][protected-use] [Snapshot cut][snapshot-cut]

An unfinished external tool/environment action with unknown effect status is similarly unavailable for an exact continuation claim unless the accepted recovery rule establishes it. No generic rollback, safe reissue or exactly-once guarantee follows from incomplete local evidence. [Unfinished external interaction][unfinished]

### 4.3 Restore identity and establish fresh readiness

Restore correspondence uses saved authority-qualified identity, semantic membership, accepted definitions/revisions, dependencies and installed arrangement. It does not use tensor order, authored address equality or wrapper identity. Required dynamic policies, wiring and grants must be reconstructible without the former authoring object; already-installed transformations must not execute twice because new Python objects are created. [Restoration][restore-readiness] [Participant identity][participant-restore] [Optimization continuation][optimization]

New physical objects can realize the same participant and optimization incarnations in a new execution session. Saved handles and readiness do not become current by loading them. Required owner reconstruction and preparation establish fresh evidence before affected use; a backend/world-size remapping requires supported correspondence. A late earlier-session result is admitted only through a valid recovery/admission rule, not because an address still matches. [Restore readiness][restore-readiness] [Preparation][preparation]

Required reconstruction failure gates dependent work. Restored model/optimizer state cannot be combined with fresh input/adaptive defaults while claiming same-run continuation. Optional sampling readiness need not block unrelated training whose requirements are satisfied. A separately requested new run has new identities and lineage; it is not an implicit failure fallback. [Failure gating][restore-failure] [Participant identity][participant-restore]

- **Metadata production:** `MetadataSnapshot`/`MetadataStoreSnapshot` contain collected records, events, edges and, at the backend boundary, required facts. Artifact projections scope records/edges to selected context. These are metadata read views, not model or optimizer reconstruction payloads. The current Trainer's pre-write metadata assembly does not establish post-write result completeness. [Snapshot definitions][metadata-code] [Store view][metadata-store] [Projection scope][projection-code] [Trainer boundary][trainer-save]
- **Cache production:** the VAE signature uses configured source and local file path/size/mtime plus padding mode; skip-existing can deliberately check only existence. The dataloader reads cached outputs and checks selected token metadata. These mechanisms do not themselves establish the target's full accepted dynamic provenance/admission or cross-owner continuation guarantees. This observation is a migration limit, not a general cache-quality finding or a claim that all legacy caching is invalid. [Current cache selection][cache-code] [Current loading][loader-code]

### 7.3 Verified areas and remaining coverage limits

The inspected requirements consistently separate semantic payload, physical resource, durable publication, observation and readiness. They also preserve partial/uncertain effects; avoid inferring rollback or replay safety from missing completion; permit compatible differing owner coordinates; require backend-complete protected reads; distinguish installed topology from construction-time callbacks; and require fresh restore readiness without recovering the authoring object. No additional mandatory semantic relationship was established as absent in these areas.

This is not exhaustive verification. I did not execute crash/rank-loss injection, remote publication, retention races, asynchronous device capture, distributed optimizer restore, world-size migration, mixed-source recovery or external-environment reconstruction. I did not audit every current writer/cache/backend, certify metadata delivery or task-review evidence, inspect unseen corrections, or assess the combined sufficiency of all research cases. No permanent dependency availability, automatic rollback, exactly-once delivery or bit-identical replay is claimed.
