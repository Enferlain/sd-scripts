Lumi's Analysis: Why the Bias?

  • • **Timestep Sampling Distribution (The Bar Chart):** This shows that during training, we intentionally sample **high timesteps ($t$)** (lots of noise) much more often than low timesteps (subtle noise).
  • • **The Hard Part:** High-noise steps are where the model has to predict *a lot* of noise from scratch. Low-noise steps are easier because the image is almost complete.
  • • **Optimization Trick:** By sampling the hard, high-noise steps more frequently, we make sure the model is *really* good at the tough parts, leading to much faster convergence and better quality images! It's like focused training!
